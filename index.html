01. About Silly Goose Woodworking

Welcome to Silly Goose Woodworking! We specialize in handcrafted wooden lanterns, featuring seasonal and custom interchangeable faceplates. We also craft home decor, custom coasters, gift card ornaments, and boxes for keepsakes or any occasion. Every piece is crafted with care, combining quality woodworking with precision laser cutting and engraving. We would love to make something for you!


Custom orders are handled through email, while our suite of standard products is available through our Etsy shop.
